# Profitable consumer AI apps run by 1–2 people (US market) and their EU/NL gap potential

Research date: 2026-11-24. Method: web search only (about 21 searches). Direct page fetches of trustmrr.com and stealwhatworks.com were **blocked by the session's network allowlist**, so TrustMRR figures below come from search-result snippets, not the verified dashboards themselves. Almost all revenue figures are **founder-reported or from aggregators** (Starter Story, Latka, Stork, Substack newsletters). None are audited. Treat them as order-of-magnitude signals.

---

## Q1. Which small-team consumer AI apps have reported revenue, and what are the details? (company list)

### Takeaway
The best-documented cases are an AI calorie tracker (Cal AI, which started with 2 founders and then grew to about 17–30 staff before MyFitnessPal bought it in March 2026), Blake Anderson's portfolio (RizzGPT/Plug AI, Umax), Pieter Levels' solo web app Photo AI, Quittr (2 founders), and a long tail of solo looksmaxxing, language and "glow-up" apps. All of them monetize through subscription paywalls and grew through TikTok/UGC or influencers, not paid search. Only Photo AI and FaceKit are clearly still run by 1–2 people. Cal AI and BetterPic both grew past the 2-person limit.

### Inferences
- Of the "1–2 person" stars, **only Photo AI (solo, web) and the FaceKit/Canary tier are still truly tiny**. Successful duos (Cal AI, BetterPic) hired once revenue passed several $M.
- Revenue decays fast in trend-driven niches (Umax down about 52% from peak; FaceKit for sale within months). Many of these apps are short-lived "trend arbitrage" rather than durable businesses.
- "Built with AI tools / vibe coding" is rarely documented explicitly. The common thread is a thin wrapper around an LLM or vision API (GPT-4o-style) plus a strong paywall and onboarding. QUITTR's "10 days, $3K" is the clearest quick-build claim.

### Gaps
- Exact launch dates are missing for most apps (Cal AI is probably about mid-2024, inferred from "$1M in 4 months" and "18 months to acquisition"). Umax and RizzGPT launched about 2023, based on the RizzGPT App Store ID range, but this is not confirmed.
- The specific build stack (Cursor, Lovable, FlutterFlow, etc.) was not found for most apps.
- TrustMRR's verified Stripe/RevenueCat numbers could not be read because the host is blocked. Allowing trustmrr.com would allow verification of FaceKit, RizzGPT and the looksmaxxing apps.
- No solid small-team examples were found for AI plant/pet identifiers, AI journaling or AI homework helpers. That market is dominated by larger companies (e.g. Gauth/ByteDance), but this was not researched in depth.

## Q2. What monetization and acquisition did they use?

### Takeaway
The pattern is consistent: free download, then a long personalized onboarding quiz, then a hard paywall with weekly, monthly or annual plans (often a 3-day trial). Distribution runs on TikTok, through founder-led accounts, paid UGC micro-creators, faceless or **AI-generated influencer** accounts, and larger influencer deals. Paid ads are layered on only after organic winners are found.

### Cited Findings
- Cal AI optimized onboarding tests and influencer marketing — [Founderpath](https://founderpath.com/resources/zach-yadegari-cal-ai-onboarding-tests-and-influencer-marketing/watch).
- FaceKit: weekly, monthly or yearly plans. Growth from about 40 TikTok accounts of AI-generated influencers — [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/blog/this-ai-trick-made-100k-heres-how).
- GoTall: test formats organically, then pay creators to replicate them, then put ads behind the winners — [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/blog/the-100kmonth-tiktok-app-idea).
- A UGC creator system that drives two apps to about $150K/month each — [Profitable Founder](https://www.profitablefounder.xyz/blog/ugc-creator-system).
- RizzGPT/Plug AI: freemium, subscriptions only, influencer-led — [VentureRadar](https://ventureradar.substack.com/p/this-23-year-olds-ai-app-makes-200kmonth); [Stormy.ai](https://stormy.ai/blog/riz-gpt-influencer-marketing-case-study).
- Rizz-category apps typically gate chat behind premium, with a 3-day free trial — [App Store: Rizz AI – Flirt Dating Pro](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6740455364).
- Industry data: AI apps earn about 41% more per user but churn about 30% faster. Monthly plans make up 59.8% of AI-app subscriptions vs 26.2% for non-AI apps. 12-month retention on monthly plans is 6.1% for AI apps vs 9.5% for non-AI apps — [PPC Land summarizing RevenueCat](https://ppc.land/ai-apps-earn-41-more-per-user-but-churn-30-faster-revenuecat-finds/).
- Weekly plans take nearly half of all subscriptions. **Europe has the highest weekly price (about $8.3, +12.2% YoY)** — [Adapty State of In-App Subscriptions 2025](https://uploads.adapty.io/state_of_in_app_subscriptions_2025.pdf).
- Median Day-35 download-to-paid conversion: North America 2.6% vs **Western Europe 2.0%** — [PPC Land summarizing RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://ppc.land/ai-apps-earn-41-more-per-user-but-churn-30-faster-revenuecat-finds/).

### Inferences
- A Dutch or EU copy of these apps would see roughly 25% lower conversion than in the US (2.0% vs 2.6%) but can charge similar or higher weekly prices. Small language markets limit the absolute volume of TikTok reach.
- The AI-influencer/faceless model is cheap to localize: Dutch or German AI avatars and voiceovers. This is an inference, not seen in practice in my sources.

### Gaps
- No NL-specific conversion or LTV data was found.

## Q4. Which EU-specific factors matter?

### Takeaway
Four factors matter most. First, smaller language markets mean less reach. Second, Western Europe converts somewhat worse than the US but supports high weekly prices. Third, stricter subscription consumer law applies: the German cancellation button since 2022, the EU withdrawal button from 19 June 2026, and the proposed Digital Fairness Act. Fourth, GDPR special-category data applies to health, biometric and sex-life apps. Ad costs in Europe appear lower than in the US, but NL-specific data is noisy.

### Cited Findings
- **EU withdrawal button:** Directive (EU) 2023/2673 requires an online withdrawal function. It was to be transposed by 19 Dec 2025 and applies from **19 June 2026** (Germany: §356a BGB) — [Churnkey](https://churnkey.co/guides/eu-consumer-rights-directive); [Global Law Experts](https://globallawexperts.com/news/eu-cancellation-button-agb-changes-what-german-businesses-must-do-by-june-19-2026).
- **Germany's "Kündigungsbutton"** (§312k BGB) has applied since 1 July 2022. Without it, consumers can cancel at any time without notice. France also requires easy cancellation. There is no EU-wide rule yet — [Churnkey](https://churnkey.co/guides/eu-consumer-rights-directive); [EVZ (German consumer centre)](https://www.evz.de/en/topics/internet-shopping/online-shopping/online-subscriptions/).
- **Digital Fairness Act** (proposed) targets free trials that auto-convert, cancel buttons and fake discounts. This hits the typical "3-day trial then weekly" paywall directly — [Inside Privacy (Covington)](https://www.insideprivacy.com/consumer-protection/digital-fairness-act-series-topic-4-digital-subscriptions/); [byburk](https://letters.byburk.net/p/digital-fairness-act).
- A reported CJEU ruling (C-234/25, 9 July 2026) said personalized services count as "digital services", which would make sign-up waivers of the 14-day withdrawal right unlawful. **This is unverified** and comes from secondary sources only — [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320108/20260710/eu-court-streaming-personalization-makes-sign-cancellation-waivers-unlawful.htm); [unsubscribetool](https://unsubscribetool.com/guides/eu-subscription-withdrawal-rights/).
- **Conversion and pricing:** Western Europe D35 conversion is 2.0% vs 2.6% in North America — [PPC Land/RevenueCat](https://ppc.land/ai-apps-earn-41-more-per-user-but-churn-30-faster-revenuecat-finds/). ### Inferences
- GDPR Art. 9 (health, biometric data used for identification, sex life) likely applies to calorie/health, face-scan and quit-porn apps. That requires explicit consent, a DPIA and EU-hosted or contractually covered processing by US LLM providers. This is a legal inference and was not verified with a lawyer or source in this session.
- Weekly auto-renewing trials that rely on dark patterns are the most exposed to the German, French and future DFA rules. An EU-compliant version should use annual plans with clear cancellation.
- Lower EU CPMs partly offset lower conversion and smaller audiences. In NL specifically, the TikTok creator pool and audience is small, so UGC volume tactics (40 accounts, 1,300 videos) saturate faster.
